# Public Assets Folder

Upload your custom sound files here so that they can be loaded by the game's audio engine!

## Supported Filenames
The game checks for custom sound files in the following priority order:
1. `jump.mp3`
2. `jump.wav`
3. `splash.mp3`
4. `splash.wav`

## How to upload
1. In the file tree/explorer of your Google AI Studio editor, find the **`public`** directory.
2. Drag and drop your audio file (e.g. `jump.mp3` or `splash.mp3`) directly into the `public` folder.
3. Reload the browser preview. The game will automatically detect and play your uploaded sound instead of the procedural synthesized sound when wildebeests or zebras jump and splash into the river!
